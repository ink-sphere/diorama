# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A person reading their locally parsed ebooks on a laptop, or a narrow browser
window, for an uninterrupted stretch of reading.

## Product Purpose

Open books from Diorama's local library, navigate their actual literary
hierarchy, and read the original XHTML with its illustrations and verse intact.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, literate, precise. Controls recede once reading begins.

## Anti-references

No analytics-dashboard furniture, promotional heroes, simulated page curls,
decorative motion, or card-within-card interfaces.

## Design Principles

- The book takes precedence over the application.
- The contents reflect the source hierarchy at every depth.
- Resume reading without reconstructing your previous context.
- Use familiar controls that work with a keyboard and on small screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, semantic nested navigation, adjustable text,
light/dark themes, reduced motion, and responsive content. Preserve language,
direction, meaningful images, and accessible markup from the book.
